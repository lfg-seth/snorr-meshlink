## Eleventh and twelfth repeaters

The study screened 3,923 physical grid locations near the two Lee roads and carried twelve new locations into the full profile search. With the ten fixed it scored 23 gateway-connected singleton additions and 265 connected pairs. The first winter trial is Lee Junction South. It gives Sisters a path to Lee and lifts SR 156 from 80.0% to 98.8%. The original 1.19 km blind stretch is covered and the longest remaining SR 156 gap is 0.12 km. Adding Macks Mouth North lifts Macks Canyon from 28.6% to 38.3%, recovers 0.52 km and reduces its longest blind stretch from 3.39 to 1.84 km. Both trial approaches fit within Fletcher's modeled one-way distance and ascent. Their new radio links clear 60% of the first Fresnel radius.

If a maintainable winter approach is found, Lee Meadows East followed by Macks North Ridge offers 3.55 km across the two roads, including 1.64 km on Macks. Its longest remaining Macks gap is 0.52 km at 59.4% reach. The critical 5.70 km relay link clears bare terrain by only 1.4 m, with 7% minimum first-Fresnel clearance. Substituting Sisters East Ridge for the twelfth site yields 100% SR 156 and 51.1% Macks coverage with stronger modeled clearance. With just one unrestricted extra slot, Lee Bowl East scores 99.6% on SR 156 and leaves the Macks result unchanged. Its current mapped approach is beyond the winter benchmark.
