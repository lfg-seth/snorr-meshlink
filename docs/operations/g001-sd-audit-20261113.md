# G001 retrieved SD-card audit - 2026-11-13

Physical file retrieval and the core capture categories pass. This is **not a
complete logging or unattended-operation acceptance**: the capture reports
record loss and abnormal resets, and some transport decisions have no recorded
reason. The longest boot provides about 7 hours 49 minutes of gapless event
sequences; it does not establish a day-long run, physical reception completeness,
or survival of every event at power loss.

## Continuity, failures and remaining capture gaps

**Good sustained evidence.** Boot `5e0dea14`, segments `00000026` through
`00000029`, contains **41,160 consecutive sequences**, with no missing or
duplicated event sequence. Observed UTC spans **2026-11-13 08:18:30-16:07:53**
(**00:18:30-08:07:53 Pacific**). Segments roll near the one-MiB limit and preserve
earlier history while space remains. This is size segmentation; no
capacity-pressure eviction occurred. FAT timestamps include unset-clock dates
and must not replace embedded times or recording serial order.

**Known dropped records.** Three boots omit **49 event sequences** in total,
exactly matching their explicit cumulative-loss increments:

| Segment | Missing sequences | Recorded loss |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `00000012.SML` | 2-8 | 7 |
| `0000002A.SML` | 34-39 | 6 |
| `0000002F.SML` | 2-37 | 36 |

No other interior sequence gaps or duplicate sequence numbers were found.
The markers do not distinguish queue overflow from unmounted/write-failed
storage; the source uses one loss counter. They cannot count a final unsynced
tail lost before the next durable record. Thus **49 is known loss, not a bound
on all possible loss**. Three successful raw reads end their respective boots
without a retained decode/duplicate outcome; do not invent those outcomes.

**Reset evidence needs follow-up.** Boot reset fields report **four panic,
two brownout, one other-watchdog and one interrupt-watchdog reset**. Several
occur after the long run. The remaining reasons are 29 unknown, five software
and four power-on. A boot field describes the preceding reset and does not
identify its root cause. No crash backtrace, watchdog task/stack, or detailed
power-fault diagnostic is stored in SML. SD alone cannot distinguish a
firmware fault, power/wiring issue, or deliberate bench trigger.

**RF outcome coverage is incomplete.** Six TX-start records lack a matching
completion. Five are within 205 ms of the last retained event of their boot;
one is in the middle of the gapless long run (`00000027.SML`, sequence 18964).
The card records no matching completion or explicit abort reason for that
transmission. Its actual outcome is **unknown**, not automatically failed.
No TX queue eviction or radio input-queue discard was observed, so those
failure hooks were not physically exercised by this sample.

**MQTT early-return reasons are missing.** **86 intents** have no envelope or
terminal skip reason, despite retaining their received decoded/raw packet.
Source has policy/configuration early returns after the intent hook, but the
specific reason is not recorded. These are distinct from the 86 opaque decode
outcomes; equal counts do not imply the same packets. **33 envelopes** lack a
publish result: 32 are explicitly queued; the remaining envelope immediately
precedes the six-record loss in segment `0000002A`. Delivery remains unknown.
No queue-full discard occurred. Reconnect attempts/errors and most serial
diagnostics are not archived as distinct events; the connectivity bitmask is
the retained connection-state evidence. No durable upload/backfill is implied.

**Configuration and reader limits.** Boot payloads retain firmware, region,
preset, requested TX power, hop limit, role, rebroadcast mode and device report
interval. They do not contain a complete non-secret configuration snapshot,
configuration-change trail, or explicit initial storage-state field. Later
health packets provide storage observations. The current reader understands
every event ID and human-decodes positions, telemetry, SNSD, SNWF and USB
survey data; SNNS/SNGH and other application types remain available as raw
application bytes rather than fully expanded neighbor/backhaul fields.
Decoded admin contents are deliberately excluded to protect credentials.

## Verification and next action

The independent Python parser and **current `origin/main` reader at `406a396`**
agree on all 61,311 records, the absence of corruption and the single open tail.
Current-reader processing reports zero unknown event IDs and zero application
decode errors for supported types. All **four existing reader tests pass**,
including Wi-Fi and the large synthetic archive. The older root checkout's
reader predates Wi-Fi support; use the current published reader when inspecting
this card. Document/whitespace checks pass for this record change.

Next useful work is a selected **G001 reset/recording diagnosis**, with power
conditions and deliberate bench triggers recorded and serial crash evidence
captured privately. Then close the transport skip/abort and diagnostic gaps
before calling the recorder comprehensive. That is a separate implementation
assignment; this audit does not flash hardware or change capture behavior.
Controlled outage, card absent/removed/write-failed/full, queue pressure,
power-cut tail-loss measurement and a day-long run remain unverified on this
physical card. Antenna A/B, calibration, field coverage and solar endurance
remain separate gates.
