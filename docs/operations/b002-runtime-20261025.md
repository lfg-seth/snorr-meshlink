## Interpretation and next measurement

This is encouraging endurance evidence: the verified boot uptime alone exceeds
the four-hour minimum and twelve-hour target numerically. It does **not** close
the [phone-free GPS/runtime acceptance](../../.scratch/tasks/gh-2-run-plan.md),
which also needs agreed start/end conditions, fresh GPS fixes and delivery
criteria. These queries do not verify GPS freshness during the run or runtime
until shutdown; remaining battery life cannot be inferred from voltage alone.

The owner suspects the absence of BLE/Wi-Fi connections helped runtime.
Record this as a **hypothesis**, not an isolated measured cause. Wi-Fi disabled
is supported by the prior saved profile; no BLE connection is owner-reported
and does not establish that the BLE radio was disabled. A comparison run with
matching battery, GPS workload, radio traffic and conditions would be needed
to quantify the connection-state effect. Next useful evidence is a later
same-boot uptime report and the eventual battery-only end time, retaining
GPS freshness and any interruptions separately.
