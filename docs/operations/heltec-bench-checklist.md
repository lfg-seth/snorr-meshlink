## Run sequence

1. **Identify and provision:** inspect one V4/one V3, choose matching stock images, establish USB/app access, configure private channel and compatible radio settings. Start the V3 with LoRa transmit disabled and channel downlink disabled. Save sanitized configuration references and keep channel keys private.
2. **Phone-free GPS:** configure GPS enabled and genuine GPS measurement timestamps. The earlier suggested diagnostic profile is 60-second acquisition, 120-second periodic reporting and smart broadcasting off; it remains a proposal, not a saved/approved profile or a reporting guarantee. Obtain a fix with sky view, remove the V4's phone connection, power-cycle it, then log stationary and moving fixes received at the V3. Record source measurement time, receiver receipt time, validity and gaps. A cached location or phone-supplied coordinate does not pass.
3. **Persistence / failure:** read settings after reboot; interrupt GPS availability and confirm old fixes are identifiable by age. Verify the V3 still receives while transmit is disabled, and check its silence across reboot. Absence of MQTT packets is not RF-silence evidence; document the observation method and its limits.
4. **MQTT uplink:** privately configure each tested V3 with its own account, `mqtt.snorr.org:8883`, TLS, root `snorr/pilot`, channel uplink on, downlink off, JSON off, map reporting off and direct Wi-Fi networking. Enable/check the V4 member's `lora.config_ok_to_mqtt`: this private broker has a public endpoint, so member forwarding must satisfy the public-IP uplink gate. Check channel position precision is sufficient for the test. Confirm MQTT encryption mode matches the subscriber's decoding plan. Verify actual protobuf position delivery to a private subscriber, not just CONNECT or gateway map reports. Add the second V3 only after the first works; record origin/packet identity, both gateway receipt times and duplicates. Do not claim the portal has ingested them.
5. **Outage / runtime:** disconnect internet and confirm local radio positions continue without a member phone. Test Wi-Fi/broker recovery. Measure V4 battery runtime under the saved GPS/reporting profile: minimum 4 hours, target 12. Record acquisition time, update-age distribution, missing reports and runtime; quantitative delivery/fix-time thresholds remain to agree before declaring an acceptance test passed.

The later designated V3 may transmit portal replies, but this initial uplink test keeps downlink disabled. Its eventual transmit behavior and the other V3's continued silence need separate verification. Add RAK relays after direct V4-to-V3 reporting works; keep survey firmware work deferred.

## Pinned firmware MQTT limits

At the checked release, `MQTT.cpp` logs MQTT username/password during direct connection and calls `setInsecure()` for direct TLS. TLS encryption therefore does not demonstrate broker certificate/hostname authentication on this client. Use isolated bench credentials and private logs; redact secrets before sharing evidence. Verified validating TLS from another client does not fix the Heltec behavior. Resolve this before treating public gateway provisioning as operationally secure. [Exact source](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/blob/54e0d8d0ab2ff56b3a9ce967e53f79e49af560fb/src/mqtt/MQTT.cpp#L328).
