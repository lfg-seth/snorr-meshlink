## Funding evidence packet

Build toward a concise packet containing the need, pilot scope, measured outcomes with limits, expense ledger and receipts, existing/in-kind contributions, equipment ownership, a supported expansion budget, and maintenance responsibility. Confirm the applicant, appropriate programs, and stakeholder requirements before drafting a specific application.

Publishing social content, contacting stakeholders, and submitting funding applications are separate actions to authorize when the material is ready.
